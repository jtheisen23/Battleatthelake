The teams

How points work

Two-man best ball, hole by hole. Low net wins the two points on each hole, and real (gross) birdies and eagles earn bonus points on top.

1 pt

Low man

Each team's best net score on the hole. The lower one wins the point.

1 pt

Team total

Both partners' net scores added together. The lower total wins the point.

1 pt

Birdie

Every gross birdie by any player earns a point for that player's team. Strokes don't count.

2 pts

Eagle

Every gross eagle or better earns two points for that player's team. Strokes don't count.
